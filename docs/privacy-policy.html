1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to anyone who creates an account or otherwise uses the LOVEWANSHI Milan app. If you only visit our website without creating an account, only the "Information we collect" section on device and log data applies to you.

2. Information we collect

Everything below is either something you typed in yourself, a photo you chose to upload, or something your device sends automatically so the app works. We do not buy data about you from anyone else.

Account information

  • Email address and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) — or, if you use Sign in with Google, your Google account's name, email address and profile photo, as shared with us by Google.

Profile information

Everything you choose to add to your profile, which is used to build the profile other members see. This is a matrimony app, so this list is intentionally broad — it includes:

  • Identity & basics: full name, gender, date of birth, marital status, height, weight, complexion, blood group, any disability you choose to disclose.
  • Contact: mobile number, WhatsApp number, present and permanent address, city, state, country.
  • Photos: the profile pictures you upload.
  • Religious & astrological details: gotra, aakna, mother tongue, place and time of birth, zodiac sign, and Manglik status — used to generate the optional birth chart (kundali) feature and for horoscope-based matching.
  • Education & career: education, employer, role, profession, annual income.
  • Family: parents' names, occupations and contact details, and the number of married/unmarried siblings, where you choose to add them.
  • About you: your self-description and partner preferences, in your own words.

Fields marked required must be filled in before your profile becomes visible to other members; everything else is optional and left to you.

Usage information

  • Who you've viewed, been viewed by, sent a connection request to, or been sent one from, and whether it was accepted.
  • Profiles you've shortlisted.
  • In-app notifications generated by the above.

Device & technical information

  • A push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver alerts like a new connection request — only if you allow notifications.
  • Basic request metadata (IP address, timestamps) generated automatically by any web request, kept in standard server logs.

We do not request your device's precise location (GPS), and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind — there are none built into the app.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your profile, and to show it to other members according to your visibility settings.
  • To power matching and search — browsing, filtering, and suggested profiles.
  • To generate your kundali (birth chart) when you ask us to, from the birth details you provided.
  • To send you notifications about activity relevant to you (a view, a request, an acceptance).
  • To verify your identity, secure your account, and prevent abuse or fake profiles.
  • To respond when you contact support.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

RecipientWhat they getWhy
Other members Your visible profile fields, per the app's visibility rules — contact details (mobile/WhatsApp/address) are only shown once you've connected with someone, not to every visitor. That's the point of the app — finding a match.
Amazon Web Services (S3) Your uploaded photos. Photo storage. Photos are not publicly listed — they're served through short-lived signed links our backend generates, not a public URL anyone can guess.

We share data with law enforcement or regulators only when legally required to.

5. How long we keep it

While your account is active, we keep your data so the app works. If you hide your profile, it stops appearing to other members but the data is unchanged and you can unhide it any time.

If you delete your account (in-app or by request — see below), your profile is immediately removed from search and from every other member's view. Some records — like the fact that a connection request existed between you and another member — are technically retained for a short period afterward, because deleting them outright would corrupt the other member's own history. This is a soft delete, not a data purge.

If you ask us to permanently erase your data (rather than just deactivate it), we will delete your personal information from our production systems within 30 days of a verified request, except where a record must be kept longer to meet a legal obligation.

6. Your choices

  • Edit any profile field at any time, from the Edit Profile screen.
  • Hide your profile from other members without deleting your account, from Account & Settings.
  • Delete your account in-app from Account & Settings, or without installing the app at all — see our account deletion page.
  • Turn off notifications from your device's system settings at any time.

7. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS); the release build refuses to send data over an unencrypted connection.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • Photos are stored privately and served through time-limited signed links rather than public URLs.
  • Access to production data is limited to the people operating the service.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — but we treat the sensitivity of what you share with us accordingly.

8. Children

LOVEWANSHI Milan is a matrimony service intended for adults. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18. If we learn that we've collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continuing to use LOVEWANSHI Milan after a change means you accept the updated policy.
