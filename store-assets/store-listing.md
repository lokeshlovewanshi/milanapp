## 📋 How to Apply in Google Play Console

### Method A: Replace Main Store Listing with Hindi (Quickest)
1. Go to **Google Play Console** ➔ **Lodha Parinay**.
2. Go to **Grow** ➔ **Store presence** ➔ **Main store listing**.
3. Under **App details**:
   - Paste the **Hindi Short description**.
   - Paste the **Hindi Full description**.
4. Click **Save** ➔ **Send changes for review**.

### Method B: Support BOTH English & Hindi Automatically (Recommended)
1. In **Main store listing**, keep English as default.
2. At the top of the page, click **Manage translations** ➔ **Add translation**.
3. Choose **Hindi (hi-IN)**.
4. Paste the Hindi App Name, Short Description, and Full Description.
5. Click **Save** ➔ **Send changes for review**.
*(Result: Hindi users see Hindi, English users see English!)*
