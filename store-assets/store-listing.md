## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Gahoi Milan is a matrimony app built for the Gahoi community — a focused,
family-first space to find a life partner, not an endless swipe feed.

VERIFIED, FAMILY-FIRST PROFILES
Every profile carries the details families actually ask about: education,
career, income, family background, and gotra — alongside the basics like
age, height and marital status. Profiles go through a review step before
they're visible, so you're browsing real people, not empty accounts.

BIRTH CHART & HOROSCOPE MATCHING
Generate your kundali (North Indian birth chart) right in the app from your
date, time and place of birth — no separate astrologer visit needed. Check
Ashtakoota compatibility (guna milan) against a profile you're interested
in before you take the next step.

BUILT FOR HOW INDIAN FAMILIES SEARCH
Search and filter by age, height, marital status, manglik status,
profession and more. Shortlist profiles to revisit later, see who's viewed
you, and send a connection request when you're ready — contact details
stay private until both sides agree to connect.

HINDI WHERE IT MATTERS
Key screens and actions are labelled in Hindi alongside English, so the app
reads naturally for the people actually filling in a family's details.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Photos are never posted to a public URL — they're served through
time-limited signed links only to people you allow. Your phone number and
address stay hidden until you connect with someone. You can hide your
profile at any time without deleting it, and delete your account
in-app whenever you choose.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Build a complete profile: basic details, education & career, family
  background, religion & astrology, contact info
• Generate and view your kundali birth chart
• Browse and filter profiles with the criteria that matter to you
• Shortlist profiles and revisit them later
• Send and receive connection requests
• See who's recently viewed your profile
• Get notified the moment someone connects with you

Gahoi Milan is free to use, with a Silver and Gold membership for members
who want unlimited requests, priority visibility and advanced filters.

Questions or feedback: jeevanmilansathi@gmail.com
```
