Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

Published: September 2026 · Android package com.lovewanshi.jeevanmilansathi

Zero Tolerance Policy: Lodha Milan has zero tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to ensuring our platform remains safe, compliant with all national and international child protection laws, and strictly restricted to adult matrimonial searches.

1. Strictly 18+ Platform (Prohibition of Minors)

Lodha Milan is an adult-only matrimonial service strictly for individuals seeking marital alliances who are 18 years of age or older.

  • Anyone under the age of 18 is strictly prohibited from creating an account, browsing, or using the service.
  • Every profile requires explicit verification of date of birth during registration.
  • Any account found or suspected to belong to a minor, or depicting a minor, will be terminated immediately and banned permanently.

2. Standards Against Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE)

We strictly prohibit the creation, uploading, sharing, storing, or transmission of:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form (photos, videos, text, or illustrations).
  • Any form of predatory behavior, solicitation of minors, sexual grooming, or child exploitation.
  • Photos featuring children or minors, even if uploaded as family photos, to prevent any misuse.

3. Content Moderation & Enforcement

To uphold these standards, our platform employs:

  • Mandatory Profile Review: All new profiles and photo uploads go through admin moderation before becoming publicly visible in search feeds.
  • Immediate Removal: Any content violating child protection policies is removed immediately upon detection.
  • Account Termination: Accounts associated with violations are permanently disabled and banned from re-registration.

4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

We urge our users and the public to report any suspected child safety violations or underage accounts immediately.

You can report concerns via:

  • Dedicated Safety Email: lovewanshisamaj@gmail.com
  • Developer Contact: jeevanmilansathi@gmail.com
  • In-App Support: Open the app ➔ Go to Help & Support ➔ Submit a Ticket.

All reports regarding child safety are treated with the highest priority and investigated within 24 hours.

5. Reporting to Law Enforcement Authorities

In full compliance with applicable child protection laws, when we become aware of apparent CSAM or child exploitation on our platform, we will promptly report the incident, user details, and associated data to relevant national and regional authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Cyber Crime law enforcement agencies.
