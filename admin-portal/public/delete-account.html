Delete your account

Two ways to do this — from inside the app, or from right here without installing anything. Both remove your profile from the app the same way.

Route 1

From inside the app

  1. Open Lodha Milan and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the menu, then Account & Settings.
  3. Tap Hide/Delete Profile, then Delete my account.
Route 2

By email — no app or login required

Send a deletion request from the email address your account is registered with, to:

Email jeevanmilansathi@gmail.com →

Include your registered mobile number if you have it — it helps us find the right account quickly if you have more than one email on file. We process email requests within a few business days and will confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted, and what's kept

Data What happens
Your profile, photos, and all fields you filled in Removed from search and from every other member's view immediately. Permanently erased from our production systems within 30 days.
Your generated kundali (if any) Deleted with the rest of your profile.
Records of past connection requests, views, and shortlists involving your account Kept in a de-identified form for a short transitional period — deleting them outright would corrupt the history on the other member's side of the interaction. These are not shown as your profile once you've deleted your account.
Login credentials Deleted — you cannot sign back into a deleted account. Signing up again means starting a new profile from scratch.

Full details on what we collect and why are in our Privacy Policy.
